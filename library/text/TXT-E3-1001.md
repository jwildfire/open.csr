Of the {{ard:t-disposition:randomised:n;group=Total}} patients randomised and
treated, {{ard:t-disposition:completed:n;group=Total}}
({{ard:t-disposition:completed:p;group=Total;scale=100;digits=1}}%) completed the
study and {{ard:t-disposition:discontinued:n;group=Total}}
({{ard:t-disposition:discontinued:p;group=Total;scale=100;digits=1}}%) discontinued
prematurely.

Completion was substantially lower in both xanomeline groups than in the placebo
group. {{ard:t-disposition:completed:n;group=Placebo}} of the
{{ard:t-disposition:randomised:n;group=Placebo}} patients randomised to placebo
({{ard:t-disposition:completed:p;group=Placebo;scale=100;digits=1}}%) completed the
study, compared with {{ard:t-disposition:completed:n;group=Xanomeline Low Dose}} of
{{ard:t-disposition:randomised:n;group=Xanomeline Low Dose}}
({{ard:t-disposition:completed:p;group=Xanomeline Low Dose;scale=100;digits=1}}%) in
the xanomeline low dose group and
{{ard:t-disposition:completed:n;group=Xanomeline High Dose}} of
{{ard:t-disposition:randomised:n;group=Xanomeline High Dose}}
({{ard:t-disposition:completed:p;group=Xanomeline High Dose;scale=100;digits=1}}%) in
the xanomeline high dose group. Premature discontinuation was therefore about twice as
frequent in the xanomeline groups as in the placebo group, and this imbalance is the
dominant feature of study conduct.

Death was recorded as the reason for discontinuation in
{{ard:t-disposition:disc_death:n;group=Total}} patients:
{{ard:t-disposition:disc_death:n;group=Placebo}} receiving placebo,
{{ard:t-disposition:disc_death:n;group=Xanomeline Low Dose}} receiving xanomeline low
dose and {{ard:t-disposition:disc_death:n;group=Xanomeline High Dose}} receiving
xanomeline high dose. The remaining
{{ard:t-disposition:disc_other:n;group=Total}} discontinuations are recorded in the
analysis dataset as other or not specified, so no further breakdown of discontinuation
reason is available from these data; the adverse event profile that plausibly accounts
for the imbalance is described in {{xref:section:12.2}}.

Patient disposition is summarised in {{xref:display:t-disposition}}. Individual
discontinued patients, with the date and the recorded reason for discontinuation, are
listed in {{xref:section:16.2.1}}.
