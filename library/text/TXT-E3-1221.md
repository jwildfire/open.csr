Treatment-emergent adverse events were reported by
{{ard:t-ae-overview:any_ae:n;group=Total}} of the
{{ard:t-ae-overview:any_ae:N;group=Total}} patients in the safety analysis set
({{ard:t-ae-overview:any_ae:p;group=Total;scale=100;digits=1}}%), covering
{{ard:t-ae-overview:n_events:n;group=Total}} individual adverse event records.

The proportion of patients reporting at least one treatment-emergent adverse event
rose with dose: {{ard:t-ae-overview:any_ae:p;group=Placebo;scale=100;digits=1}}% of
patients receiving placebo,
{{ard:t-ae-overview:any_ae:p;group=Xanomeline Low Dose;scale=100;digits=1}}% receiving
xanomeline low dose and
{{ard:t-ae-overview:any_ae:p;group=Xanomeline High Dose;scale=100;digits=1}}% receiving
xanomeline high dose. The same gradient was present, and more pronounced, for adverse
events assessed by the investigator as related to study drug, reported by
{{ard:t-ae-overview:related_ae:n;group=Placebo}} patients
({{ard:t-ae-overview:related_ae:p;group=Placebo;scale=100;digits=1}}%),
{{ard:t-ae-overview:related_ae:n;group=Xanomeline Low Dose}} patients
({{ard:t-ae-overview:related_ae:p;group=Xanomeline Low Dose;scale=100;digits=1}}%) and
{{ard:t-ae-overview:related_ae:n;group=Xanomeline High Dose}} patients
({{ard:t-ae-overview:related_ae:p;group=Xanomeline High Dose;scale=100;digits=1}}%)
respectively.

Most events were of mild or moderate maximum severity. Severe events were reported by
{{ard:t-ae-overview:sev_severe:n;group=Placebo}} patients
({{ard:t-ae-overview:sev_severe:p;group=Placebo;scale=100;digits=1}}%) receiving
placebo, {{ard:t-ae-overview:sev_severe:n;group=Xanomeline Low Dose}} patients
({{ard:t-ae-overview:sev_severe:p;group=Xanomeline Low Dose;scale=100;digits=1}}%)
receiving xanomeline low dose and
{{ard:t-ae-overview:sev_severe:n;group=Xanomeline High Dose}} patients
({{ard:t-ae-overview:sev_severe:p;group=Xanomeline High Dose;scale=100;digits=1}}%)
receiving xanomeline high dose; moderate events were reported by
{{ard:t-ae-overview:sev_moderate:n;group=Total}} patients
({{ard:t-ae-overview:sev_moderate:p;group=Total;scale=100;digits=1}}%) overall.

Serious adverse events were uncommon in this study. They were reported by
{{ard:t-ae-overview:serious_ae:n;group=Total}} patients
({{ard:t-ae-overview:serious_ae:p;group=Total;scale=100;digits=1}}%) overall:
{{ard:t-ae-overview:serious_ae:n;group=Placebo}} receiving placebo,
{{ard:t-ae-overview:serious_ae:n;group=Xanomeline Low Dose}} receiving xanomeline low
dose and {{ard:t-ae-overview:serious_ae:n;group=Xanomeline High Dose}} receiving
xanomeline high dose. Adverse events with a fatal outcome were recorded for
{{ard:t-ae-overview:fatal_ae:n;group=Total}} patients, of whom
{{ard:t-ae-overview:fatal_ae:n;group=Placebo}} were receiving placebo. Deaths and
serious adverse events are described in {{xref:section:12.3}}.

An overview of treatment-emergent adverse events is presented in
{{xref:display:t-ae-overview}}. Because exposure differed substantially between the
groups ({{xref:section:12.1}}), the crude proportions above should be read alongside
the exposure summary.
