Demographic and baseline characteristics were comparable across the three treatment
groups in the safety analysis set of
{{ard:t-demographics:age:N;group=Total}} patients.

The study population was elderly, as expected for a mild to moderate Alzheimer's
disease population. Mean age overall was
{{ard:t-demographics:age:mean;group=Total;digits=1}} years
(SD {{ard:t-demographics:age:sd;group=Total;digits=2}}), with a median of
{{ard:t-demographics:age:median;group=Total;digits=0}} years and a range of
{{ard:t-demographics:age:min;group=Total}} to
{{ard:t-demographics:age:max;group=Total}} years. Mean age was
{{ard:t-demographics:age:mean;group=Placebo;digits=1}} years in the placebo group,
{{ard:t-demographics:age:mean;group=Xanomeline Low Dose;digits=1}} years in the
xanomeline low dose group and
{{ard:t-demographics:age:mean;group=Xanomeline High Dose;digits=1}} years in the
xanomeline high dose group.

Women accounted for {{ard:t-demographics:sex:n;variable_level=F;group=Total}} patients
({{ard:t-demographics:sex:p;variable_level=F;group=Total;scale=100;digits=1}}%) overall, and the
proportion of women ranged from
{{ard:t-demographics:sex:p;variable_level=F;group=Xanomeline High Dose;scale=100;digits=1}}% in
the xanomeline high dose group to
{{ard:t-demographics:sex:p;variable_level=F;group=Placebo;scale=100;digits=1}}% in the placebo
group. The population was predominantly white
({{ard:t-demographics:race:n;variable_level=WHITE;group=Total}} patients,
{{ard:t-demographics:race:p;variable_level=WHITE;group=Total;scale=100;digits=1}}%), with
{{ard:t-demographics:race:n;variable_level=BLACK OR AFRICAN AMERICAN;group=Total}}
patients reported as Black or African American. The limited racial diversity of the
population should be considered when generalising the findings of this study.
