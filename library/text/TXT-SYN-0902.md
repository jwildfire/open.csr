Safety was evaluated from treatment-emergent adverse events, coded to system organ
class and preferred term, and summarised by treatment group; from serious adverse
events, deaths and adverse events leading to withdrawal; and from the extent of
exposure to study drug.

Laboratory values, vital signs and concomitant medications are collected in the
source study and are analysed in the reference clinical study report for this
dataset, but no analysis results datasets for them exist in this framework yet, so
they are not evaluated here.
