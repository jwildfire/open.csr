Exposure to study drug was longest in the placebo group and shortest in the xanomeline
low dose group, a direct consequence of the discontinuation pattern described in
{{xref:section:10.1}}. The imbalance must be taken into account when interpreting crude
adverse event frequencies.

Mean duration of exposure was
{{ard:t-exposure:duration:mean;group=Placebo;digits=1}} days
(SD {{ard:t-exposure:duration:sd;group=Placebo;digits=2}}) in the placebo group,
{{ard:t-exposure:duration:mean;group=Xanomeline Low Dose;digits=1}} days
(SD {{ard:t-exposure:duration:sd;group=Xanomeline Low Dose;digits=2}}) in the
xanomeline low dose group and
{{ard:t-exposure:duration:mean;group=Xanomeline High Dose;digits=1}} days
(SD {{ard:t-exposure:duration:sd;group=Xanomeline High Dose;digits=2}}) in the
xanomeline high dose group. The corresponding medians were
{{ard:t-exposure:duration:median;group=Placebo;digits=1}},
{{ard:t-exposure:duration:median;group=Xanomeline Low Dose;digits=1}} and
{{ard:t-exposure:duration:median;group=Xanomeline High Dose;digits=1}} days.
Individual exposure ranged from
{{ard:t-exposure:duration:min;group=Total}} to
{{ard:t-exposure:duration:max;group=Total}} days across the safety analysis set.

Cumulative exposure thresholds show the same pattern. At least 30 days of exposure was
achieved by {{ard:t-exposure:dur_30:n;group=Placebo}} patients
({{ard:t-exposure:dur_30:p;group=Placebo;scale=100;digits=1}}%) receiving placebo,
{{ard:t-exposure:dur_30:n;group=Xanomeline Low Dose}}
({{ard:t-exposure:dur_30:p;group=Xanomeline Low Dose;scale=100;digits=1}}%) receiving
xanomeline low dose and {{ard:t-exposure:dur_30:n;group=Xanomeline High Dose}}
({{ard:t-exposure:dur_30:p;group=Xanomeline High Dose;scale=100;digits=1}}%) receiving
xanomeline high dose. Long-term exposure of at least 180 days was reached by
{{ard:t-exposure:dur_180:n;group=Placebo}} patients
({{ard:t-exposure:dur_180:p;group=Placebo;scale=100;digits=1}}%) in the placebo group,
against {{ard:t-exposure:dur_180:n;group=Xanomeline Low Dose}}
({{ard:t-exposure:dur_180:p;group=Xanomeline Low Dose;scale=100;digits=1}}%) and
{{ard:t-exposure:dur_180:n;group=Xanomeline High Dose}}
({{ard:t-exposure:dur_180:p;group=Xanomeline High Dose;scale=100;digits=1}}%) in the
low and high dose groups respectively — so fewer than half as many xanomeline patients
as placebo patients reached the long-term exposure threshold.
