Dermatologic events are the adverse events of special interest the study flagged before unblinding — preferred terms judged dermatologic in nature, such as rash, pruritus and dermatitis. The event time is the day of the first such event; subjects without one are censored at their study completion date (ADTTE CNSR = 1).
Kaplan-Meier estimates by treatment group, with the median and its 95% confidence limits on the linear (Brookmeyer-Crowley) scale, and the log-rank test across the three groups. The log-rank test is a single result for the whole display and is printed once.
The median was not reached in the placebo group — fewer than half of those subjects had a dermatologic event — so its median and confidence limits are empty rather than a number.
The curve and the numbers at risk are drawn from the same committed analysis results dataset as the statistics beneath them: the survival estimate at each event time and the counts at 0, 30, 60, 90, 120, 150 and 180 days are rows of that dataset, not a second calculation. Individual censoring times are not marked on the curve; they are not carried in the analysis results dataset, and the count of censored subjects is reported in the table instead. The submission RTF carries the statistics only.
The log-rank chi-square and its degrees of freedom are reported by open.csr and are not in the published figure, which states only that the difference between groups was significant. They are shown so that the p-value beneath them can be checked rather than taken on trust.
A p-value smaller than the precision this display declares is reported at that boundary — '<0.0001' — rather than rounded to '0.0000', which would assert a probability of zero. The unrounded value is retained in the analysis results dataset.
