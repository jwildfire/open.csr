Windowed rows include only assessments that fell inside the visit's assessment window, and impute nothing; a subject with no assessment in that window contributes to none of the statistics for that row, so n is below the column N. LOCF rows carry the last observation forward, so every subject contributes at every visit and n equals the column N.
The baseline row of each visit is the mean baseline score of the subjects contributing to that visit, not the baseline of the whole column. The two differ wherever a visit is missing for some subjects, which is why the windowed rows are the ones that move.
